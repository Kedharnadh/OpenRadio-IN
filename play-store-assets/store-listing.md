# Play Store Listing — OpenRadio-IN v1.6.0

## English (US)

**App name:** OpenRadio-IN

**Short description (<=80 chars):**
Free Indian radio: music, news, talk & sports. Android, Auto, TV, Chromecast.

**Full description:**
OpenRadio-IN is a fast, free internet radio app that brings hand-picked Indian online radio stations to your device — music, news, talk, devotional and sports — all in one place.

FEATURES
• Curated Indian stations across Telugu, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Bengali, Marathi and more, plus genres like devotional, news and sports.
• Instant search and filters — find a station by language, state or genre in seconds.
• Favourite stations, recents and sleep timer.
• Wake up to your favourite station with the built-in alarm.
• Reliable playback: buffering and error retry are handled automatically, with an HLS proxy for streams that need it.
• Plays in the background with lock-screen, notification and Android Auto media controls.
• Cast to Chromecast devices, or run it on Android TV.
• Offline-ready station list — browse even when the connection is weak.
• Light, dark and material-you themes, with a wide-screen layout for tablets.

OpenRadio-IN contains no ads and requires no account. It only needs internet to stream.

Works on Android 8.0 (API 26) and above. Station streams belong to their respective owners; OpenRadio-IN only lists publicly available streams.

Feedback or station suggestions? Reach us via the app or at github.com/Kedharnadh/OpenRadio-IN.

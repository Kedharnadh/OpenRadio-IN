Privacy Policy

OpenRadio-IN

Last updated: 11 September 2026

1. Overview

OpenRadio-IN (“the app”, “the website”, “we”) lets you discover and stream online radio stations. This policy explains what information is collected, why, and how you can control it. The app and website do not create accounts, collect names, email addresses, or payment information, and they do not sell or share personal data to third parties.

2. Station data

The list of radio stations is fetched from a public GitHub repository (github.com/Kedharnadh/OpenRadio-IN) and cached on your device for offline use. No personal data is included in this request. Your IP address is visible to our hosting providers only as a normal part of serving content.

3. Analytics & crash reporting

We use analytics and crash-reporting tools to understand how the app is used and to fix problems. These services collect aggregated, non-personal diagnostic information:

  • Android app — Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics. This may include crash logs, performance data, device type, OS version, and device identifiers used for analytics (e.g. an advertising/analytics ID). This is managed by Google and subject to Google's privacy policy. You can consent to, or disable, analytics in the app's settings screens.
  • Website (PWA) — Google Analytics (property G-48120XZZQ4), which may use cookies to measure anonymous usage.

4. Audio proxy

Some streams are played through an HLS proxy hosted on Cloudflare Workers (openradio-hls-proxy.kedharnadh1.workers.dev). The proxy only relays audio and does not log or store personal data beyond transient connection metadata.

5. Services we rely on

The app uses third-party services that process minimal technical data: Google Play Services (Cast), Cloudflare (hosting and the audio proxy), and GitHub Pages (station data hosting). Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy.

6. Permissions

The Android app requests only the permissions needed for playback:

  • Internet — to stream radio stations;
  • Foreground service + media playback — to keep playing while the screen is off;
  • Notifications — to show playback controls and a “now playing” notification.

7. Data retention & deletion

The app stores only your local preferences (favourite stations, volume, alarm) on your device. To delete this data, clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Diagnostics forwarded to Google Analytics/Firebase are retained under the retention settings of those services; aggregated data is kept for up to 14 months and can be disabled as described in section 3.

8. Children

The app is a general media player and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Changes & contact

We may update this policy from time to time; changes appear on this page. Questions? Open an issue at github.com/Kedharnadh/OpenRadio-IN/issues.
